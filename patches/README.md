## CBUG-0062 left-hand ring attacks

A Warrior overlooks a charged fire ring on the left hand and leaves an ogre
at 70 HP. With both ring checks corrected, the same ring attack activates the
left ring and kills the ogre. Changing either check alone still misses the
attack. Five controls, including right-ring preference and recharge rejection,
are unchanged. This isolates a ring attack; it does not measure a full fight
or player harm.

Source [issue78](https://github.com/diegoami/neo-angband-tracker/issues/78),
C source `46c14b2828ed751ff8be7ac67df93b28cdc15f87` against pristine C
`e473720ed88a0f0c7ca66815cef3000eb4a38bc3`.
[Stored patch](upstream-c/CBUG-0062-both-ring-slots.patch) and
[readable comparison, partial alternatives, replay and Verification](../docs/audit/c-reports/CBUG-0062/README.md).
This stored proposal is not source adoption or an engine/consumer correction.
