## Caster counted as blocking its own projectile (issue279)

The Borg rejects a hill giant's unobstructed boulder at its starting square
and rates it at zero danger, yet the native boulder removes18 HP. The helper
correction recognizes the shot; the resulting teleport costs2mana and still
takes29 HP in this seeded case. The storm-giant open-path example instead
uses a scroll and avoids54 HP of scheduled damage. These are bounded outcomes,
not uniform safety. Player-origin paths, endpoints and walls remain valid;
real intermediate monsters still block the shot.
