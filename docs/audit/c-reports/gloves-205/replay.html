Gloves could never be chosen as the armour swap

The Borg keeps a situational armour swap: when the main gear lacks a protection the situation needs, it scores every wearable pack item and carries the best one. The scoring switch covered rings, amulets, boots, helms, crowns, shields, cloaks and body armour — but not gloves, so a pair of gauntlets of Free Action was never eligible, however badly the Borg needed free action. The correction adds gloves to the switch.

What the controls establish

Plain gauntlets stay a non-swap, and a carried free-action pair stays a non-swap when free action is already worn — both with byte-identical logs — so the correction changes only the free-action-needed case.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, seed 1000, glowing constructed arena, declared deepest level 60 (swaps are only carried past 50), full hp. Free Action was granted to the fixture's gauntlets by flag, matching what an ego or artifact pair carries. The production armour-swap scan runs directly; its tracked swap and flags are read back, nothing is worn or executed in the game. This isolates the swap eligibility, not full autonomous play; no survival claim.
