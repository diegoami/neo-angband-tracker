Vampire Strike was judged by a bystander's distance

The Borg scans its target list for the closest monster to strike, but the closeness test afterwards reads a variable that the scan left holding the distance of the last entry it examined — including entries it skipped. One distant bystander anywhere in the list kept the spell from ever being cast, even with a perfect target five grids away.

What the controls establish

With both monsters close, both versions cast; with only the distant monster, both refuse. Those two complete logs match byte for byte, so the correction changes nothing except the bystander case.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Necromancer level 26, depth 30, seed 1000, lit constructed arena, the Fear and Torment shadow book, Vampire Strike learned as the only spell, full mana, a torch. The production attack chooser acts on its own filled target list; the cast is shown by its queued keys, not executed. This isolates the closeness guard, not full autonomous play; no damage or survival claim.
