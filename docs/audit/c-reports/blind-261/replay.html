The fear spell that promised confusion

The Borg has a dedicated Frighten maneuver, scored as the fear effect it is. A second maneuver - named for blinding a creature - also casts Frighten but was scored with the confusion attack type. Against monsters whose danger the model suppresses under confusion but not under fear, the mislabeled maneuver carried a phantom value the spell cannot deliver, high enough to win the chooser and be cast.
