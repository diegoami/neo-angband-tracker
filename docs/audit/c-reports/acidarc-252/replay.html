Recorded native Acid Spray decisions

Ball and arc blasts never counted their south and east edges

The Borg's blast loops ran from center minus radius up to but excluding center plus radius, so any monster standing exactly radius grids south or east of the blast never entered the scan. Acid Spray is scored as a player-centred cone with a ten-grid radius: a hill giant standing exactly ten grids south contributed nothing, and with it the only live attack the Borg rated the whole situation as not worth a cast. The correction makes the loops inclusive; the existing radius distance check already keeps grids beyond the radius out.

What the controls establish

With the giant eight grids south (inside the loop either way), both versions cast with identical targeting; with nothing in sight, both refuse. Those two complete logs match byte for byte, so the correction changes only the edge case.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Mage level 50, depth 30, glowing constructed arena, the Attacks and Knowledge book carried, Acid Spray learned and the only live attack (20 mana blocks Frost Bolt at cost 40), full hp, seed 1000. The production attack chooser acts on its own filled target list; the cast is shown by its queued keys, not executed. This isolates the blast-radius scan, not full autonomous play; no damage or survival claim.
