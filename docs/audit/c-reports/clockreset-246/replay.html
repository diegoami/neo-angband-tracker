The clock-overflow abort fired before the graceful reset

The borg clock sits exactly on the abort threshold (BORG_TIME_MAX − 3000). The original deactivates the borg there — about 2900 turns before the graceful reset in borg_think can ever fire — so the borg stops for good and the clock stays frozen. The correction resets the clock in place and keeps playing; the overflow check in borg_think remains as a failsafe.
