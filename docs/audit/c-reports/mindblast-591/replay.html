Recorded native C runs, original and corrected source

The Borg valued a Mind Blast at a quarter of its damage

A Mind Blast deals 8d8 damage (average 36, at most 64) and then confuses for 3+1d4 turns. The Borg danger estimate valued it at the caster spell power divided by two, plus one: 9 for Nar, 15 for a mind flayer, 21 for a beholder, and ignored the confusion. The correction values it at the 8d8 maximum, as the other spells are, and adds the usual concern for the confusion.

Nar casts Mind Blast (8d8, up to 64) among other spells. Original: the Borg values the Mind Blast at about 9. Corrected: at 64, so Nar is rated by his strongest attack.
