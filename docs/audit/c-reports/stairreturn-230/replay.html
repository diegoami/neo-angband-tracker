The out-of-light stair command was queued and then disowned

When the Borg is out of light and standing on a staircase, its routine queues the stair key (<). But the branch fell through without returning, so the routine reported "nothing done" — and the think loop, believing nothing was queued, kept queueing more commands behind the stair key. Everything after < would land on the new level after the stairs were taken. The correction returns true after queueing the stair command.

What the controls establish

With a healthy torch and with a dead torch away from stairs, both versions behave identically — leave it alone, find nothing — with byte-identical logs, so the correction changes only the on-stairs case.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, depth 30, seed 1000, glowing constructed arena, the worn torch's fuel set per situation, spare torches and the recall scroll removed from the pack (recorded as a fixture intervention) so the wear-stuff and recall branches have nothing to act on. The production out-of-light routine runs directly; its queued keys are shown, not executed. This isolates the missing return, not full autonomous play.
