Stacked with #146: which grid the searching Borg walks to

Issue #147's slip (the secret-door search scores a candidate grid with its north-west neighbour's search count) only steers the Borg once issue #146 is corrected, because on the pristine source the search can only choose the Borg's own grid. This run applies #146's correction alone, then #146's and #147's corrections together, and lets the bored Borg search and walk along a granite wall. With #146 alone, after its first search the Borg picks the grid next to it, which that search already covered, and creeps one grid at a time, searching every grid twice. With both corrections it picks a grid no search has covered yet and moves on two grids at a time. The pristine source is shown for reference: it never leaves its grid.

After searching at (35,2) the counts of (34..36, 1..3) are 5. With #146 alone the score reads each candidate's north-west neighbour: (34,2) is scored with (33,1)'s 0, so the Borg steps west onto a grid it has just covered, searches it again, and repeats: six searches, five on covered grids, in twelve calls. With both corrections (34,2) and (36,2) carry their own 5, so it walks to (37,2), which no search has covered, and continues to (39,2) and (41,2): four searches, none repeated. The pristine source searches its own grid three times and never moves.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, seed 147, level 20, depth 5, alone in a closed 22x70 room with a granite north wall, no stairs or monsters, just arrived on the level (level timer 0). 'wall': the Borg starts against the north wall at (35,2) and the production borg_flow_spastic(true) (the bored call) runs twelve times in a row.
