The drained Borg power-dived anyway

The “do not dive when drained” guard was gated on g, the direction chosen before the check: g is only non-zero when an earlier branch already decided to head up. For a drained Borg that had not yet chosen a direction the guard was dead, and the power-dive branch (“playing too shallow”) fired anyway. The correction applies the drained guard unconditionally, like the sibling “full of items” guard.
