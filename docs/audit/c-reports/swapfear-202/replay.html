Recorded native C runs, original and corrected source

A fear-protecting spare weapon got no credit, so the Borg kept the wrong one

When the Borg carries more than one spare weapon it keeps the most useful as its swap weapon and offers the others to the shops. Its scoring adds a bonus for a swap that protects from fear when the Borg has no fear protection of its own, but the flag that bonus reads was never set from the weapon, so a fear-protecting weapon was scored as if it gave nothing. The correction sets that flag (and the matching armour flag) from the item, exactly as protection from confusion and blindness already are.

A level-29 Warrior with no fear protection of its own carries two spare Maces: one protects from fear, the other has one extra point of damage. Original: the fear protection is never recorded, so the +1 Mace scores higher, is kept as the swap weapon, and the fear-protecting Mace is offered for sale. Corrected: the fear-protection bonus applies, the fear-protecting Mace is kept and the +1 Mace is offered for sale.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, seed 202, character level 29 (30 in the brave control, where the Warrior class gives its own fear protection), deepest level 50 (the Borg only carries a swap weapon from level 50), no gold, a torch and rations, in a lit room, every rune known. Fixture interventions: the starting Potion of Berserk Strength is removed (it is the cheapest spare and would be offered first); the two spare Maces are made directly, one with the PROT_FEAR object flag set (the flag the (Holy Avenger) and of Fury egos and 26 artifacts carry) and one plain with one extra point of damage. The production swap scan, borg_power() and the shop sell-useless decision run unchanged. No combat, fear episode or death is measured.

Canonical report: issue 202.
