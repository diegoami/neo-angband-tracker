The Teleport Other skip list reads one entry too far

A level-30 Mage considers Teleport Other on depth 100 with a kobold beside it and a hill giant to the other side. The Borg's Morgoth-depth pass stores the giant in the second slot of its skip list; the regular evaluation then targets the kobold, and the original danger check walks one entry past the list and also suppresses that leftover giant. The cast is valued as if it removed both monsters.

What the controls establish

Without the Morgoth-depth pass the second slot is never written and both versions agree. When only one monster exists, the leftover slot holds that same monster, so a duplicate is harmless. Only the two-monster sequence with the deep pass first changes the value.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Mage level 31, depth 100, seed 1000, lit constructed arena, Teleport Other known, full mana for the regular evaluation and 30 mana during the Morgoth-depth pass. The Borg's own depth-100 assumption that Morgoth is present is set directly, matching what borg-update.c derives there. The production launch calls from both Teleport Other defenses are used unchanged; the target-candidate list is filled the same way the defense fills it.

This isolates the skip-list read, not full autonomous play or monster AI casting. Danger figures are the Borg's heuristic, not HP predictions. No death or survival claim.
