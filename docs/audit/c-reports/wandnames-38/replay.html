Recorded native C runs, original and corrected source

Two wands were looked up under the wrong names, so the Borg aimed the wrong wand

When the Borg starts it looks up each wand it knows how to use by name. Two lines were copies of the lines above them: the Confuse Monster wand was looked up as Scare Monster, and the Lightning Balls wand as Lightning Bolts. So the attack chooser offered a Lightning Bolts wand (a 6d6 beam, about 21 damage) as a 64-damage ball, offered a Scare Monster wand as a confusion bolt, and never used a real Lightning Balls or Confuse Monster wand. The correction looks the two wands up by their own names.

The Borg carries a Lightning Bolts wand (6d6 beam, about 21 damage) and a Frost Bolts wand (12d8 bolt, about 54 damage). Original: the Lightning Bolts wand is also offered as a Lightning Balls attack worth 64 damage, so the Borg chooses it and would fire a 21-damage beam. Corrected: the Lightning Bolts wand is offered only as a beam and the Borg chooses the Frost Bolts wand.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, seed 38, character level 20, dungeon level 20, in a lit arena, every rune known, an awake black ogre (100 hit points, no electricity or cold immunity) four grids east in a straight line. Fixture interventions: every starting pack item except food is removed so the wands under test are the only ranged attacks; the wands are made directly with 10 charges and are fully known; the ogre is entered into the Borg monster list through the production observe_kill_diff(). The production borg_calculate_attack_effectiveness() scores each wand attack and the production borg_attack() chooses and queues the attack; the zap itself is not played.

Canonical report: issue 38.
