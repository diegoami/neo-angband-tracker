Recorded native Dispel Life ratings and casts

The Borg rated Dispel Life against nobody

The game's Dispel Life hits every living monster in line of sight. The Borg's rating kept damage only for monsters flagged both nonliving and undead — the exact inverse — and since no monster in the game data carries the nonliving flag, the spell was valued below doing nothing against every enemy. Against this hill giant a native cast removes 66 HP.

What the controls establish

An undead vampire bat is immune in the game and both versions refuse the cast; the native cast removes nothing. The primary nether bolt's complete log is byte-identical before and after the correction: only the Dispel Life filter changed.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Necromancer level 26, depth 30, seed 1000, lit constructed arena, the Fear and Torment shadow book, Dispel Life and Nether Bolt learned, full mana, a torch. The production rating call and its damage expression are used unchanged; the native cast goes through the game's own command and its damage is measured on the monster's hit points. This isolates the rating filter, not full autonomous play; damage rolls are random, the rating is the average expression. No death or survival claim.
