Excessive danger never made an ordinary Borg flee

When the danger at the Borg's grid is more than twice its hit points, it should start fleeing — the comment adds 'do not flee level if going after Morgoth'. The condition instead required the Borg to be ready for Morgoth, so ordinary Borgs never fled here. A level-40 Warrior on 8 hit points with a cave bear beside it and an up staircase two steps away therefore did not flee: it tried to teleport (none carried) and read Word of Recall, staying next to the bear. With the owner-decided correction (flee unless going after Morgoth) it starts fleeing, and the dash for the staircase two steps away applies.

Original: no excessive-danger flee; teleport fails and the Borg reads Word of Recall beside the bear. Corrected: '# Fleeing (excessive danger)', then 'Desperate for Stairs (two)', a step west toward the staircase (key 52).

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, seed 156, level 40, depth 30, starting kit (no teleport, one Word of Recall), a lit room, an awake cave bear adjacent (Borg danger estimate 20), an up staircase two steps west, tracked by the Borg. Main case 8 hit points (danger above twice the hit points); 'hurt' control 15 hit points (danger between one and two times); 'morgoth' case 8 hit points with the Borg marked ready for Morgoth (an artificial setting at depth 30, used only to show the intended reversal). The production borg_caution() runs once and the first queued key is read back. This isolates the escape decision; it does not play out the turns that follow.
