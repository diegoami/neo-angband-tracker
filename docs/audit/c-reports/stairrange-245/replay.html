The shrunk stair range stopped the munchkin climb

The munchkin stair-range adjustment adds (clevel − 10) + (10 − depth) to the base range of 8. Below character level 10 the first term is negative, so the “increase” shrinks the range: a level-5 borg at depth 10 gets range 3, and its known staircase five grids away no longer counts as close. The correction gates the adjustment on clevel > 10, restoring the base range below level 10.

The original falls back to the generic any-stair flow (“Munchkin Mode. Any Stair.”); the corrected borg makes the targeted climb decision (“Munchkin Mode. Power Climb.”).

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, seed 245, level 5, depth 10, no monsters, a known up staircase five grids north. The borg’s own tracked staircase list is seeded to match a viewed staircase; the production munchkin decision runs once and the goal state plus borg notes are read back directly. This isolates the range arithmetic and the climb decision; it does not measure autonomous play.
