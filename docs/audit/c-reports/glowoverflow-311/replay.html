The light list that outgrew its shelf

When the Borg is in the dark it flows back towards remembered light, listing every perma-lit grid on the screen into two arrays of 1536 entries - with no bound. A fully lit panel holds more than that.

What the control establishes

A panel with a thousand glowing grids fits the arrays in both variants - glow_n 1000, byte-identical logs. The correction changes only what happens past the bound.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior on a fully perma-lit arena (the Borg map marked BORG_GLOW throughout), seed one thousand, terminal sized so the panel sits inside the map; the production out-of-light flow runs in full. The memory-safety proof is the same run built with AddressSanitizer (strcmp interceptor disabled - the pristine base trips an unrelated interceptor read while sorting monster names at init). The measured fact is the unbounded write and the overflow; what the write corrupts afterwards is build-dependent and not measured here, and the live out-of-light trigger is the supplied evidence, not claimed here. No death or survival claim.
