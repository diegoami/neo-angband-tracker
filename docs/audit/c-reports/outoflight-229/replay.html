A light about to burn out never started the out-of-light maneuvers

The Borg keeps a routine for running low on light: swap to a spare, refuel, recall, or take stairs. Its entry test read "no light, AND (no light or fuel within three turns)" — the first part already forces the second, so a lit lantern three turns from dying never started the routine, and the Borg walked into darkness with a fresh torch in its pack. The correction reads "no light, OR (fueled light within three turns of dying)", with fuel-less (everburning) lights excluded so their permanent zero timeout cannot false-trigger it.

What the controls establish

With plenty of fuel, with a fuel-less (everburning-style) light, and with a fully dead light, both versions behave identically — skip, skip, and swap respectively — with byte-identical logs, so the correction changes only the dying-light case.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, depth 30, seed 1000, glowing constructed arena, a worn lantern whose timeout is set per situation and a flask of oil plus the starting torch in the pack. The production out-of-light routine runs directly; its queued keys are shown, not executed. This isolates the entry gate, not full autonomous play; no survival claim beyond the routine starting.
