The Sea of Runes stalled after its centre rune

Before fighting Morgoth the Borg builds a Sea of Runes: a 5x5 block of Runes of Protection with itself at the centre. To choose the next rune grid it measures how far every grid is from where it stands. That measurement was asked to stop early, and because it starts at the Borg's own grid at distance 0 it stopped before measuring any other grid. Once the centre rune is laid and the Borg stands on it, the centre is excluded and no other grid looks reachable, so the Borg does nothing more on the Sea. The correction measures the whole neighbourhood, and the Borg steps toward the next rune grid.

Original: two further calls both return false with no key queued; only the Borg's own (excluded) grid has a distance, so no outlying grid is chosen. Corrected: the first further call flows toward a ring grid at distance 1 and queues a step (key 51, south-east).

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, seed 345, level 50, depth 99, a full-size open level (198x66), 40 known Rune of Protection scrolls. The production borg_flow_glyph() runs as the Borg would call it: the first call chooses the Borg's own grid as the centre, the second call arrives and reads a Rune of Protection (the fixture then lays that rune on the grid, the result of the read), and up to two further calls must choose and act on an outlying rune grid. The off-centre control starts those later calls one grid east of the centre, on an unglyphed ring grid. This isolates the rune-grid choice; it does not run a whole Sea of Runes or a Morgoth fight. The first and second calls behave identically in both versions.

Canonical report: issue 345.
