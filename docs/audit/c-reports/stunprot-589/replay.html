Recorded native C runs, original and corrected source

The Borg treated sound resistance as protection from stun

Gravity, plasma and force breath, water attacks, storms and ice bolts can stun. In the game only protection from stun stops that; sound resistance stops only the stun of sound itself. The Borg skipped its extra danger for being stunned again whenever it resisted sound, for all of these attacks, and it did not track protection from stun at all. The correction adds a protection-from-stun trait and uses it for those attacks.

The armour gives sound resistance only. Gravity breath stuns regardless. Original: the stun penalty is skipped (0) because the Borg resists sound. Corrected: the penalty applies.

Fixture interventions: one Soft Leather Armour is made directly with sound resistance and/or protection from stun and worn; all runes known; the stun timer is set; the hound enters the Borg monster list through the production observe_kill_diff(). The production borg_danger() is read at the Borg grid, then again with only the Borg stunned traits cleared; the difference is the stun penalty.
