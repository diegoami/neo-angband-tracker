The poisoned, hallucinating Borg stair-cycled

In munchkin start mode, a poisoned and hallucinating Borg on a known stair kept choosing stair travel. Stair trips do not age the world clock, so the timed ailments could never wear off. The correction makes the munchkin branch step aside while such ailments are active, handing the turn to the ordinary recovery path, which ages turns normally.

What the recorded run establishes

Two native scenarios run per version: the ailed case (poisoned and hallucinating on a known up stair at depth 9) and the clean control. The original stair-cycles in the ailed case; the corrected Borg does not, and the clean control still power-climbs. Queued keys and Borg notes are recorded for both.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, seed 1045, depth 9 (past the munchkin depth target, engaging the Power Climb branch), one known up stair underfoot and a down stair three tiles east, no monsters. The ailments enter through the real timed-effect API and traits derive through the production notice path. The fixture isolates this decision stage; it does not replay the consumer zoo run that first stalled there, and it makes no survival or encounter-frequency claim.
