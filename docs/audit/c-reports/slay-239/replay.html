The Borg forgot what its weapon could slay

The slay traits were assigned, not accumulated, for every equipment slot in the Borgs inventory scan. The last occupied slot - usually the light or the boots, which carry no slays - therefore erased whatever the wielded weapon provided, and every melee valuation saw an unslaying weapon. A Slay Animal blade was rated exactly like a plain one.

What the control establishes

Against a small kobold, which carries no animal flag, all three readings coincide and the complete log is byte-identical before and after: the correction changes only the case where the slay applies. The forced-on reading (the same attack with the slay trait set by hand) is identical in both variants, so the correction restores exactly the value the slay is worth.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior level thirty-one, depth five, seed one thousand, lit constructed arena, a Broad Sword carrying the animals slay from the slay table (race flag ANIMAL, the base multiplier of two) wielded in the weapon slot, the birth torches wielded in the later light slot, an adjacent awake white harpy (or small kobold in the control). The measurement calls the public thrust valuation with the slay trait as the inventory scan computed it, then with the trait forced off and on. This isolates the trait accumulation, not full autonomous play. No death or survival claim.
