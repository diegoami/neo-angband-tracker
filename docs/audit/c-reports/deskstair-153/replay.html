A far staircase hid the one two steps away

When the Borg is in trouble and a staircase is only a step or two away, it dashes for it. To decide, it looks up how far the nearest free staircase is. Both look-ups (up and down staircases) kept the farthest staircase instead of the nearest, so if the Borg also knew a second staircase far away, the near one did not count. A badly hurt level-5 Warrior with a cave bear beside it and an up staircase two steps away then skipped the dash and read Word of Recall, which only takes effect many turns later, while staying next to the bear. The correction keeps the nearest staircase, and the Borg steps toward it.

Original: the far staircase is kept as the 'nearest', the dash is skipped; teleport fails (none carried) and the Borg reads Word of Recall, staying next to the bear. Corrected: 'Desperate for Stairs (two)', a step west toward the staircase two squares away (key 52).

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, seed 153, level 5, depth 5, 15 of 86 hit points, starting kit (no teleport), a lit 38x22 room. A cave bear stands awake next to the Borg (south-east); the Borg's danger estimate (20) exceeds its hit points. A staircase is two steps west; in the main cases a second staircase of the same kind is 25 steps east, both tracked by the Borg. The production borg_caution() runs once and the first queued key is read back. This isolates the escape decision; it does not play out the fight or the recall.
