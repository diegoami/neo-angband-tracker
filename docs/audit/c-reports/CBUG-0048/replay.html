A blocked boulder makes the Borg dismiss the giant's area attacks

The Borg treats a storm giant's boulder as unable to reach it, then carries that decision over to the giant's lightning area attacks. In the open-path recording it stays and loses 54 HP. After the per-spell correction it uses one Phase Door scroll, moves behind a wall and keeps all 130 HP.

The mold-in-the-path example shows the limit: the correction spends a scroll but lands in another exposed square and still takes 54 HP. This is not guaranteed escape or uniform safety. A separate defect also makes the caster count as blocking its own projectile; both corrections are tested independently below.

What the alternatives establish

Correcting the projectile path alone changes the open-path example, but leaves the mold-blocked mixed attack underestimated: the Rogue still stays and takes 59 HP. Assessing each spell separately restores the area-attack estimate in both cases. The independent boulder-path problem remains outside the proposed patch; the hill giant control exposes it.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Rogue level 20, 130 HP, depth 38, seed 1000, known Phase Door spell and one scroll, constructed Intelligence/arena setup. The actual stock monster spell lists retain their normal order. One scheduled spell belongs to the actual caster: storm giant/patriarch ball of lightning, orc shaman wounding, hill giant boulder. The native active-monster context supplies the caster's real spell power.

Production danger/escape code chooses. Exact spell/book or scroll keys are validated and executed natively. Afterward one native spell effect is scheduled only if the actual caster still has range and a terrain-clear path. This selects a valid attack for the experiment; it does not run monster AI spell selection, casting failure frequency, a full player fallback turn or a whole fight. Native damage, saving throws, item use and movement are not substituted.

Different actions naturally consume different random draws; seed/setup are unchanged and RNG is not reset between decisions. The native Phase Door spell has a 5% failure chance in this setup; shown attempts succeed. A danger estimate is a heuristic, not predicted HP damage. No encounter-frequency or death claim.
