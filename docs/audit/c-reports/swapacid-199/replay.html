A lightning-resisting swap weapon counted as acid resistance

A level-50 Warrior keeps a second weapon in its pack to swap to when it needs that weapon's protection. When the Borg records what its chosen swap weapon protects against, it reads the acid entry from the weapon's lightning resistance. A dagger that resists only lightning therefore counts as acid protection, and the Borg's depth-readiness check (which demands acid protection from depth 26 down) says it is ready although nothing it owns resists acid. A dagger that really resists acid is not counted. The correction reads the acid entry from the acid resistance.

Original: the lightning-only dagger is recorded as acid protection, so the Borg believes it resists acid and the depth-30 readiness check answers ready. Corrected: acid is not counted and the readiness check answers resist acid.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, seed 199, character level 50, maximum depth 50 (the Borg only keeps swap weapons from depth 50), standing on depth 30. Worn: a Ring of Resist Fire and Cold, a Ring of Free Action, an Amulet of ESP, Metal Scale Mail of Resist Lightning, a Long Sword and a lantern; carried: food, recall, phase and teleport scrolls, cure potions. Nothing worn resists acid. The pack holds one swap weapon per scenario (Dethanc: lightning only; Angrist: acid; Narthanc: fire only; or none). All runes are learned so the Borg sees each item's real properties. The production notice pass picks the swap weapon and the production readiness check runs once for depth 30. This isolates the swap reading and the readiness answer; it does not measure autonomous play or a later acid attack.
