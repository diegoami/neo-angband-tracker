Recorded native C runs, original and corrected source

The Borg's home score ignored Scrolls of Banishment

The home score rewards stocked Scrolls of Banishment (genocide) and Mass Banishment, 'mainly used for Morgoth'. The scan that counts the home's scrolls only counted Mass Banishment, so the Banishment reward was always zero and the Borg never offered a Banishment scroll to the home. The correction counts them.

A level-26 Mage with an empty home carries a Scroll of Banishment, a Scroll of Word of Recall, a ration and a spell book. The production home-store routine decides what to put in the home. Original: the home score gives a Banishment scroll nothing, so the Borg leaves it in the pack. Corrected: the scroll is worth the same as other stocked scrolls and the Borg offers it to the home.
