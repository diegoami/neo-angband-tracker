The Borg judged Phase Door by landings that never happen

Before reading Phase Door the Borg simulates 100 landings and refuses if too many would be dangerous. It sampled landings 5 to 10 squares away. The game aims at 10 squares, randomised by up to one either way, and lands as close to that as it can: 9, 10 or 11 squares. So the Borg weighed landing spots that never occur and ignored the farthest ring. The correction samples the distance the way the game does.

A real Phase Door lands 9 to 11 squares away, never next to a mold. Original: about a third of its sampled landings are 5 to 7 squares away, next to a mold, so it refuses. Corrected: it judges 9 to 11 squares and finds Phase Door safe.

Experiment conditions and limits
Native Human Warrior, seed 595, character level 20, 3 hit points, five Phase Door scrolls, open lit arena. In the ring case, grey molds (which never move) stand on every grid exactly 6 squares away, so only landings 5 to 7 squares away are next to one. Fixture interventions: hit points set to 3 so a landing next to a mold counts as scary; the molds enter the Borg monster list through the production observe_kill_diff(). The production borg_caution_phase(20, 2), the threshold most of its callers use, runs once.
