The Borg underrated its Cure Serious Wounds potions

Before drinking a cure potion the Borg checks that the potion will heal enough to cover the danger it faces. It estimated Cure Light, Serious and Critical Wounds as a percentage of the hit points lost (at least 15, 25 and 30), the 3.x values its comments still name. In 4.2 these potions heal a fixed 20, 40 and 60. At low hit points the Borg therefore expected too little and left a potion in the pack that would have covered the danger. The correction uses the fixed amounts.

A Cure Serious Wounds heals 40, so it covers the danger. Original: the Borg expects only 25 and does not drink it. Corrected: it drinks it.
