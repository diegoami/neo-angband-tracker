The penalty that counted twice

The Borg holds back mana for a final teleport, and casting a dispel from that reserve is punished with a heavy penalty on the maneuver value. Two back-to-back blocks applied it: one keyed on the class reserve - six for a Mage, eight for a Priest, up to twenty-two for a Ranger - and a second keyed on a fixed six. The second is a subset of the first for every class, so the penalty simply doubled.

What the control establishes

With mana outside the teleport band neither block applies and both variants value the dispel identically, byte for byte: the correction removes only the duplicated penalty.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Priest level twenty-one, depth five, seed one thousand, lit constructed arena, every class book carried, wisdom maximized, three awake vampire bats in line of sight, current mana tuned to five points (or twenty in the control) above the dispel cost after casting. The measurement calls the public maneuver chooser with borg_simulate set; the raw dispel launch value in this fixture is the beam-path quirk seen elsewhere (minus one), which the penalty arithmetic dwarfs and does not affect. This isolates the penalty blocks, not full autonomous play. No death or survival claim.
