Waiting for recall, the low-mana caster never restored its mana

When the Borg has read Word of Recall and is in danger while it waits, it 'buys time'. Its first step restores mana if it is a caster below a quarter of its maximum mana, so it can cast its escapes. The test compared the current mana with a quarter of the current mana, which is never true, so the step never ran. A badly hurt level-35 Mage with 19 of 193 mana and two Restore Mana potions, a cave bear beside it, therefore did nothing here. The correction compares with the maximum mana, and the Mage quaffs Restore Mana.

Original: the mana test is never true; after the failed teleport attempt, caution takes no action (rc 0). Corrected: 'Quaffing Potions of Restore Mana' / 'Buying time waiting for Recall.(1)' (key q).

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Mage, seed 157, level 35, depth 30, 9 hit points, starting kit plus 2 Restore Mana potions, an awake cave bear adjacent (Borg danger 20, above twice the hit points), an up staircase two steps west. Word of Recall is active (the game's recall timer is set, as after reading the scroll). The Borg has not been given its spells in this fixture, so it cannot cast Phase Door; the production borg_caution() runs once and the first queued key is read back. Main case 19 of 193 mana; control 96 of 193 (above a quarter). This isolates the first 'buy time' step; it does not play out the recall.

Canonical report: issue 157.
