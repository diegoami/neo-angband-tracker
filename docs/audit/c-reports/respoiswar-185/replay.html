Resist Cold potions cancelled a warrior's Resist Poison reward

Warriors past depth 20 get an extra 'power' reward for carrying up to four potions each of Resist Heat, Resist Cold and Resist Poison. The counter was reset before the heat and cold loops but not before the poison loop, so the poison count started where the cold count stopped. A warrior with four Resist Cold potions got no warrior reward for its three Resist Poison potions: 900 instead of 2400. The correction resets the counter before the poison loop too.

Original: the warrior poison loop starts at the Resist Cold count (4) and adds nothing: 900 (the all-classes reward only). Corrected: 2400.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Warrior, seed 185, level 25, maximum depth 25 (on depth 20), a lantern, 5 Rations of Food and 3 Resist Poison potions; in the main case also 4 Resist Cold potions. The value of the poison potions is the production borg_power() with the stack minus without it (expected 3 x 300 for all classes plus 3 x 500 for warriors). The production junk pass then runs once and drops nothing in either version. Uses of the score (selling, buying, dropping when the pack is full) were not exercised.

Canonical report: issue 185.
