Four apples made Resist Poison potions worthless

The Borg judges its pack by a 'power' score; carrying up to four Resist Poison potions should add 300 each. The counter for that reward was not reset, so it started where the previous reward (low-calorie food) left it. A Mage carrying four apples therefore valued its three Resist Poison potions at 0 instead of 900. The correction starts the count at zero.

Original: the reward loop starts at the apple count (4) and adds nothing: the potions are worth 0. Corrected: 900 (3 x 300). Both versions then drop the apples as junk.

Experiment conditions and limits

Native Human Mage, seed 184, level 25, depth 20, a lantern, 5 Rations of Food and 3 Resist Poison potions; in the main case also 4 Apples (low-calorie food). The value of the potions is the production borg_power() with the potion stack minus without it. The production junk pass (borg_drop_junk) then runs once: in both versions it drops the apples first, so this fixture shows the lost valuation, not a lost potion. Uses of the score (selling, buying, dropping when the pack is full) were not exercised.

Canonical report: issue 184.
