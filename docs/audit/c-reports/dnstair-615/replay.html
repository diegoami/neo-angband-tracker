A hurt Borg on a down staircase did not take it

When the Borg is in danger while standing on an up staircase it leaves the level by it. On a down staircase danger alone did nothing: the stairs were taken only when fleeing or prepared for the next depth, so a hurt, unprepared Borg tried to teleport or read Word of Recall while staying next to the monster, although the stairs work at once. The correction lets it go one level deeper when in danger (the same test as for up staircases) on a down staircase.

Original: the stairs are not taken; teleport fails (none carried) and the Borg reads Word of Recall, staying next to the bear. Corrected: it takes the stairs (">").
