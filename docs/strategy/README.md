# The Angband Strategy Manual

*A source-code-grounded companion to the official manual, for Angband 4.2.6 (C version).*

The official manual (`docs/*.rst`: [birth](../birth.rst), [dungeon](../dungeon.rst),
[attack](../attack.rst), [playing](../playing.rst), [commands](../command.rst),
[options](../option.rst), [guide](../guide.rst)) tells you **what** the game's
commands and screens are. This manual tells you **how the game actually works
underneath** and **what to do about it**: the formulas, the monsters' real
damage output, the depths where each threat first appears, the resistances you
need before going there, and how to fight the uniques that will end your
character if you meet them unprepared.

Every number here comes from this repository: from the data files in
`lib/gamedata/` and the C code in `src/`. References like
`src/player-calcs.c:calc_blows()` point to the function to read if you want to
check, and the appendices are generated by a script directly from the data, so
they match the game exactly. If you change the data files, re-run:

```bash
python3 docs/strategy/tools/gen_appendices.py
```

## How to use this manual

* **New to Angband?** Read [Chapter 2](02-races-classes.md) to choose a
  character, then the early-game sections of [Chapter 8](08-phase-strategy.md).
  Come back to the other chapters when something kills you.
* **Planning a dive?** Look up the depth band in
  [Chapter 4 § resistance checklist](04-resistances-afflictions.md) and
  [Chapter 8](08-phase-strategy.md), then scan the
  [monster compendium](A1-monster-compendium.md) rows around your target depth.
* **A unique just showed up?** Find it in [Appendix A2](A2-uniques.md) for its
  exact HP, speed, spell list and maximum damage, and in
  [Chapter 6](06-monsters-uniques.md) for tactics.
* **Found an item?** [Chapter 5](05-equipment-objects.md) explains what is worth
  keeping; Appendices [A4](A4-artifacts.md), [A5](A5-ego-items.md) and
  [A6](A6-objects.md) list every artifact, ego type and object kind.

## Ten rules that keep characters alive

These rules are explained and justified in the chapters. They are collected
here because together they prevent most deaths.

1. **Speed is the most important stat.** At +10 a monster acts twice for
   each of your turns (Chapter 1). Never fight a fast unique without speed or
   an escape.
2. **Get Free Action before about DL20 and See Invisible soon after.**
   Paralysis with no saving throw kills characters of every level
   (Chapter 4).
3. **Always carry an escape that works when you are blind or confused**:
   potions (even Cure Light Wounds cures blindness and reduces confusion) and
   a Staff of Teleportation. Staffs, wands and rods can be used blind, but
   scrolls cannot be read (`player_can_read()` in `src/cmd-obj.c`)
   (Chapters 4 and 8).
4. **Resist the base four (fire, cold, acid, lightning) and poison before the
   30s**, and resist **confusion, blindness, nether, nexus, chaos, sound and
   shards** before diving deep. Check the
   [breath table](A3-monster-spells.md#breath-table) against your HP.
5. **A breath is the monster's current HP divided by a constant.** A wounded
   dragon breathes for much less (Appendix A3). Damage it before letting it
   breathe, or leave line of sight.
6. **Know a unique before you fight it.** Look it up in
   [A2](A2-uniques.md). If its biggest hit is more than half your HP,
   don't fight it yet.
7. **Don't fight in the open.** Corridors limit packs, hounds and breath
   cones (Chapter 6).
8. **Leave levels freely.** A bad level feeling, a dangerous unique or a pack
   of hounds you can't handle are reasons to take the stairs (Chapter 7).
9. **Identify by use, and read every rune**: the 4.2 rune system means that
   wearing items teaches you their properties (Chapter 5).
10. **Plan your escape before you need it.** Decide at what HP you will
    quaff, phase, teleport or leave the level, and act at that point
    (Chapter 8).
