Every non-unique monster in the game, sorted by native depth (DL). Columns:

* **Spd** — speed relative to normal (+10 = twice as many turns as a normal-speed character).
* **HP** — *average* hit points (non-uniques are rolled with a normal distribution, std. dev. ≈ HP/8, see `mon_hp()` in `src/mon-make.c`).
* **Blows** — melee attacks (`method effect dice`; plain damage shown without an effect).
* **Spells** — `1_IN_x` is the chance per turn to cast; numbers in brackets are the **maximum damage** of that spell for this monster: breaths use `HP / divisor` capped (`breath_dam()` in `src/mon-spell.c`) computed at *average* HP, other spells use the monster's spell power (defaults to its depth).
* **Imm/Vuln** — elemental immunities and vulnerabilities.
* **Notes** — tactically relevant flags (inherited from the monster base as well); `groups` means it appears with friends.

Use this table to answer *"what can kill me on this level?"* — scan the rows ±5 levels around your depth for big breath numbers, paralysis (`paralyze`), experience drain (`exp_*`), and stat drain.
