# Appendix A3 — Monster Spells, Breaths and Damage Formulas

> *Generated by `docs/strategy/tools/gen_appendices.py` from `lib/gamedata/monster_spell.txt, projection.txt`. Do not edit by hand; re-run the script instead.*

Monster spell damage depends on the caster's **spell power**, which is its native depth unless `spell-power` overrides it in `monster.txt` (`src/mon-init.c`). Breaths ignore spell power: damage is the monster's **current** HP divided by the element's divisor, capped (`breath_dam()`, `src/mon-spell.c`). A wounded breather is much less dangerous.

Resist fraction: damage is multiplied by numerator/denominator once per level of resistance (`adjust_dam()` in `src/project-player.c`). One permanent resistance is level 1; a timed resistance (`OPP_ACID`, `OPP_ELEC`, `OPP_FIRE`, `OPP_COLD`, `OPP_POIS`) adds one more level up to 2 (`src/player-calcs.c`), so acid/elec/fire/cold/poison drop to 1/3 with one resist and 1/9 with both. Immunity (level 3) negates the damage; vulnerability multiplies it by 4/3. Acid damage is additionally halved if it hits a piece of armour (`minus_ac()`). High elements use a random denominator `8+1d4`, so a resisted breath does 6/9 to 6/12 of its raw damage. Elements with no numerator listed (water, gravity, inertia, force, time, plasma, mana...) cannot be resisted by damage reduction at all.
